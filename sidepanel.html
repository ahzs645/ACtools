Structured client snapshot

Searches for or reads a client through the current authenticated AlayaCare session and builds a reviewable JSON snapshot. This version is restricted to UAT test data.

  • Includes demographics, medical history, risks, contacts, notes, services, care plans, forms, tasks, medications, and document metadata
  • Records the source endpoint and success or failure of every section
  • Includes attachment metadata only—never attachment file contents
  • Downloads locally and does not upload or transmit the chart
